How to play

54 cards in five shapes: circles, triangles, crosses, squares and stars, plus five wild Whot cards numbered 20. There are no 6s and no 9s.

Everyone gets 6 cards. The top card starts the call card. On your turn play a card with the same shape or the same number as the call card, play a Whot on anything, or pick from the market (that ends your turn).

  • 1 Hold on: you play again.
  • 2 Pick two: the next player picks 2 unless they defend with another 2. Each defended 2 grows the pick.
  • 5 Pick three: same, with 5s, and 3 cards at a time.
  • 8 Suspension: the next player misses a turn. A star 8 misses two players.
  • 14 General market: everyone else picks 1, then you play again.
  • 20 Whot: wild. Play it on anything and call the shape you want next.

When the market runs out, everything under the call card is shuffled into a new market. First to empty their hand wins the round. Stars count double in scoring, Whot counts 20.
